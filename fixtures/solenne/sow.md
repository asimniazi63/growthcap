# Statement of Work: Growth Reporting and Advisory

**Client:** Solenne (fictional premium clean skincare brand)
**Agency:** Growth Capital (fictional engagement)
**Statement date:** 2025-08-25
**Term:** 2025-09-01 to 2026-12-31

## 1. Scope

The agency provides weekly performance reporting and monthly planning for Solenne's direct-to-consumer Shopify store. Reporting covers sales, orders, new and returning customers, discounts and refunds. Wholesale orders are reported separately from direct sales.

## 2. Key performance targets

The targets below apply to direct sales and exclude wholesale orders.

| # | KPI | Target | Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Net sales | $26,767 in total | Q4 2026 (2026-10-01 to 2026-12-31) |
| 2 | Returning-customer rate | At least 45% | Each month in Q4 2026 |
| 3 | Refund rate (refunded units as a share of units sold) | No more than 6% | Each month, 2026-10-01 to 2026-12-31 |
| 4 | New customers during Black Friday weekend | At least 30 | 2026-11-27 to 2026-11-30 |

Monthly net sales targets that make up KPI 1 are held in the monthly plan: October $6,507, November $10,433 and December $9,827.

## 3. Definitions

- **Net sales** are gross sales minus discounts and refunds. Shipping and taxes are not included.
- **Returning-customer rate** is the share of customers with an order in the period who have ordered before.
- **Refund rate** is the share of units sold in the period that have been refunded.
- **New customers** are customers placing their first order.

## 4. Reporting

- A weekly summary is sent every Monday.
- A monthly review compares actual results with the monthly plan and explains the largest gaps.
- Targets may be changed by written agreement between the client and the agency.

## 5. Responsibilities

- Solenne provides access to its store data and confirms promotions and budget changes in advance.
- The agency provides the reports, explains variances and recommends actions.
