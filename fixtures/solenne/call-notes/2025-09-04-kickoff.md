# Solenne x Growth Capital: kickoff call

**Date:** 2025-09-04
**Attendees:** Mara Ellison (Solenne, founder), Tomas Reyes (Solenne, operations), Priya Nandakumar (Growth Capital, partner)

## Summary
Solenne sells premium clean skincare, direct to consumer, with a small wholesale side. The first goal is to grow new-customer orders without losing margin. Growth Capital will report weekly on sales, orders, new customers, returning-customer rate and refund rate against a monthly plan.

## Decisions
- Wholesale orders are reported separately from direct sales, because one large order can distort the average order value.
- The plan is set monthly, from September 2025 through December 2026.
- Black Friday is the main event of the first year.
